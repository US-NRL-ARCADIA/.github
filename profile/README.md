# US NRL ARCADIA

This organization is for the publication of ARCAIDA and related materials developed by the 
Integrated Cognitive Systems Section at the [United States Naval Research Laboratory](www.nrl.navy.mil)'s [Naval Center for Applied Research in Artificial Intelligence](https://www.nrl.navy.mil/itd/aic/). 
All materials published in this GitHub organization are approved for public
release under Distribution Statement A; distribution is unlimited.
